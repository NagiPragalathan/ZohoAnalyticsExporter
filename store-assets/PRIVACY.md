# Privacy Policy — Zoho Analytics Exporter

**Last updated: 1 August 2026**

## Summary

Zoho Analytics Exporter does not collect, store, transmit, or share any data.
Everything it does happens locally in your browser.

## What the extension does

When you open a Zoho Analytics report, the extension reads the report data that
Zoho has already sent to your browser, converts it into an Excel or CSV file,
and saves that file to your computer when you click a download button.

## Data collection

**None.** Specifically:

- No personal information is collected.
- No report data is collected, copied, or retained by the extension author.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind.
- No cookies or tracking identifiers are set.
- No browsing history or activity on other websites is accessed.

## Data transmission

**None.** The extension has no backend server and makes no network requests of
its own. Your report data never leaves your computer. The generated Excel and
CSV files are created in-browser and saved directly to your download folder.

## Data storage

**None.** The extension requests no storage permission and keeps nothing between
sessions. Report data is held in the page's memory only while the tab is open,
and is discarded when you close or reload it.

## Permissions

The extension requests no Chrome API permissions.

It requests permission to run only on Zoho Analytics domains
(`analytics.zoho.com`, `.eu`, `.in`, `.com.au`, `.com.cn`, `.jp`, `.ca`, `.uk`,
`.sa`, and `analytics.zohocloud.ca`), because reading the report you are viewing
is its entire function. It does not run on any other website.

## Third parties

The extension contains no third-party code, libraries, SDKs, or services. No
data is sold or transferred to anyone.

## Your data stays yours

The files the extension produces contain whatever your report contains, which
may include personal data about other people. Those files are yours to control —
store and share them in line with your own organisation's data-protection
obligations.
